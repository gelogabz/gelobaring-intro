02

The rest of the daylight.

The camera is only part of it. The rest is music, books, games, and the long way to a coastline.

I spend my working life making things legible: glossaries, help centers, release notes. Photography is the opposite. I point the camera at something and try not to explain it.

Most of these were taken on the way somewhere: a summit, a coastline, a road between the two. The weather did most of the work. I just had to be there early enough.

There is no client, no brief, and no analytics. If a photo is here, it is because I still like looking at it.
